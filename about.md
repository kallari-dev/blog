Recall is a notebook on offensive security, written under the handle Kallari. It holds two kinds of writing: posts that dig into one topic, and project overviews that describe something built or carried out.

## Where to find things

- **[Blog]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }})**: writeups of challenges and lab machines, guides on methodology and tools, and the occasional extra.
- **[Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }})**: overviews of things I made, from engagements to coding and research. Clients, employers and systems are never named.
- **[Portfolio]({{ '/portfolio/' | relative_url }})**: whoami, the operator profile and skill tree.
- **[GitHub]({{ site.github_url }})**: code and tools.
- **[RSS]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }})**: new posts as they land.
